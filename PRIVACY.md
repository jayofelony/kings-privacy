# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 31, 2026

Jeroen Oudshoorn ("we," "us," or "our") operates Kings (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and the third-party services used within our Game.

---

### 1. Our Data Collection Practices
We do **not** personally collect, harvest, store, or have access to any of your personal data. 
* We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, or contact details.
* We do not track or store your gameplay statistics on external servers.
* All game saves, high scores, and settings are stored strictly locally on your own device.

### 2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)
To keep this Game free to play, we display third-party advertisements using **Google AdMob**. While we do not collect your data, Google AdMob automatically collects and processes certain information from your device to serve, optimize, and measure ads. 

The data collected by Google AdMob may include:
* **Device Identifiers:** Your device's advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID on Android or the IDFA on iOS).
* **Technical Data:** Your IP address, device model, operating system, mobile carrier, and language settings.
* **Ad Interactions:** Information about which ads are shown to you, how often they appear, and whether you click on them.

To understand how Google processes and protects your data, please review their official privacy policy:
* [Google Privacy & Terms (Advertising)](https://google.com)

### 3. Your Privacy Rights and Opt-Out
Depending on your region (such as the European Union under GDPR), you have the right to opt-out of personalized advertising tracking. You can control, reset, or disable your advertising choices at any time directly through your device settings:
* **Android:** Go to *Settings > Privacy > Ads* to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
* **iOS:** Go to *Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking* to manage app tracking permissions.
